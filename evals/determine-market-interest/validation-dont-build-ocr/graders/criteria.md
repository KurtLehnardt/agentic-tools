PASS if:
- Issues DON'T BUILD verdict
- Explicitly cites Kill Gate 1 (Platform-Native) from kill-gate.md
- Names the specific native solutions (iOS Live Text, macOS Preview, Google Lens, OneNote, Google Docs)
- Acknowledges that these are pre-installed/default (no distribution disadvantage)
- Notes the market signal: declining searches after 2020 (when Google and Apple shipped native)
- Explains that you cannot out-distribute a default
- Recognizes this as a permanent kill (Gate 1 kills do not expire)
- Justifies the verdict: the gap does not exist, the need is already met at OS level

FAIL if:
- Issues BUILD, NARROW, or PIVOT verdict
- Suggests the idea survives Gate 1 (e.g., by adding features like batch processing)
- Does not cite Kill Gate 1
- Omits naming the competing native solutions
- Treats this as a viable market opportunity
- Claims a differentiator that would overcome the built-in solution
- Does not acknowledge the search-interest decline
