Please validate this idea for me: "an app that extracts text from images (OCR)."

I found these competing solutions:
- iOS Notes app: https://support.apple.com/en-us/119858 (built-in Live Text, free, on every device)
- macOS Preview: https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT211969 (built-in OCR, free)
- Google Lens: https://support.google.com/lens/answer/10607096 (built-in Android, free, on every device)
- Microsoft OneNote: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/c7e9b5d3-d5f2-4b93-9e4e-3f7a4e4e7d7e (OCR in note-taking app, free tier available)
- Google Docs: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/176472 (upload image, free, in-document OCR)

These are all:
- Free
- Pre-installed or default (no download friction)
- One-click workflows (no separate app needed)
- Cross-platform (mobile and desktop)
- Accessible to non-technical users

The idea has been around for 20+ years. The market confirms it's a solved problem — see the declining search interest for "OCR app" (nosedive in 2019-2020 when Apple and Google shipped native solutions).

Should I build this?
